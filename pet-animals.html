🏡 Meet Our Animal Friends

Explore interesting facts, habitats, food habits, behaviour, fun facts, size and lifespan of familiar pet and domestic animals.

🐶 Dog

🔬 Real Facts

  • Dogs are mammals and are closely related to wolves.
  • Dogs have an excellent sense of smell.
  • There are hundreds of different dog breeds.
  • Dogs can learn commands and many different tasks.
  • Dogs are social animals that can form strong bonds with people.

🏡 Habitat

  • Domestic dogs usually live with people in homes.
  • They can adapt to many different environments.
  • Dogs need a safe and comfortable place to rest.
  • They need regular exercise and fresh air.
  • Pet dogs depend on people for food, water and care.

🍖 What Does a Dog Eat?

  • Dog food
  • Meat
  • Rice and grains
  • Vegetables
  • Some fruits

🧠 Behaviour

  • Dogs use barking, body movements and facial expressions to communicate.
  • They can be playful and active.
  • Many dogs enjoy playing and spending time with people.
  • Dogs can be trained to follow commands.
  • They can recognize familiar people and sounds.

😂 Fun Facts

  • A dog's nose is much more sensitive than a human nose.
  • Dogs can learn many words and commands.
  • Every dog has a unique nose print.
  • Dogs use their tails to show different emotions.
  • Some dogs can be trained to help people in many useful ways.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–13 years
Weight: 1–90+ kg
Height: Varies by breed
Speed: Up to 70 km/h

🐱 Cat

🔬 Real Facts

  • Cats are small mammals that are commonly kept as pets.
  • Cats have excellent hearing and night vision.
  • They use their whiskers to sense nearby objects.
  • Cats have sharp claws that help them climb and catch prey.
  • Every cat has a unique pattern, colour or combination of features.

🏡 Habitat

  • Pet cats usually live in homes with people.
  • They can adapt to both indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Cats like quiet and comfortable places for resting.
  • They often choose high places where they can observe their surroundings.
  • Pet cats depend on people for food, water and care.

🍖 What Does a Cat Eat?

  • Cat food
  • Fish
  • Chicken
  • Meat
  • Some specially prepared pet foods

🧠 Behaviour

  • Cats often sleep for many hours each day.
  • They communicate using meows, purrs and body movements.
  • Cats like to groom themselves regularly.
  • They are naturally curious and enjoy exploring their surroundings.
  • Many cats enjoy playing with toys and people.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Cats can jump several times their own body height.
  • A cat's whiskers help it sense spaces around its face.
  • Cats can rotate their ears to detect sounds.
  • Purring can happen when cats are relaxed, but also in some other situations.
  • Cats spend a large part of their day grooming themselves.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 12–18 years
Weight: 3–7 kg
Length: 45–50 cm
Speed: Up to 48 km/h

🐄 Cow

🔬 Real Facts

  • Cows are gentle, social animals that often live in groups.
  • Cows are mammals and their babies are called calves.
  • Cows have a very good sense of smell.
  • They have four stomach compartments that help digest grass.
  • Cows can recognize familiar animals and people.

🏡 Habitat

  • Domestic cows are commonly raised on farms and grasslands.
  • They enjoy open areas where they can walk and graze.
  • Cows need clean water and a safe place to rest.
  • They often stay together as a herd.
  • Farmers provide shelter during very hot, cold or rainy weather.

🌿 What Does a Cow Eat?

  • Grass 🌱
  • Hay
  • Leaves
  • Grains
  • Other plant-based food

🧠 Behaviour

  • Cows are calm and social animals.
  • They enjoy staying close to other cows.
  • Cows spend many hours eating and chewing their food.
  • They communicate using different sounds and body movements.
  • Mother cows are protective of their calves.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Cows can sleep while standing for short periods.
  • A cow's nose has a unique pattern, similar to a fingerprint.
  • Cows can remember familiar faces.
  • Cows spend a lot of time calmly chewing their food.
  • Baby cows are called calves and are usually very playful. 🥹

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 15–20 years
Weight: 400–900 kg
Height: 1.2–1.5 m
Speed: Up to 40 km/h

🐐 Goat

🔬 Real Facts

  • Goats are friendly and social animals that often live in groups.
  • Baby goats are called kids.
  • Goats are excellent climbers and have very strong, flexible feet.
  • They have rectangular-shaped pupils that help them see around them.
  • Goats can learn to recognize familiar people and other goats.

🏡 Habitat

  • Domestic goats are commonly raised on farms and in villages.
  • They can live in grasslands, hills and rocky areas.
  • Goats are able to adapt to many different environments.
  • They need a safe shelter and clean drinking water.
  • They usually enjoy staying close to other goats.

🌿 What Does a Goat Eat?

  • Grass 🌱
  • Leaves 🍃
  • Vegetables 🥕
  • Fruits 🍎
  • Shrubs and small plants

🧠 Behaviour

  • Goats are curious and playful animals.
  • They enjoy climbing and exploring their surroundings.
  • Goats communicate using bleats and body movements.
  • They often stay together in small groups.
  • Mother goats are very protective of their kids. 🥹

😂 Fun Facts

  • Baby goats can stand and walk soon after they are born.
  • Goats are surprisingly good climbers.
  • They can balance on narrow and rocky surfaces.
  • Goats have excellent memories and can learn simple tasks.
  • A group of goats is called a herd.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–18 years
Weight: 20–140 kg
Height: 40–100 cm
Speed: Up to 24 km/h

🐑 Sheep

🔬 Real Facts

  • Sheep are gentle and social animals that usually live in groups.
  • Baby sheep are called lambs. 🥹
  • Sheep have thick wool that helps protect them from cold weather.
  • They have a very good sense of hearing and smell.
  • Sheep can recognize other sheep and familiar people.

🏡 Habitat

  • Domestic sheep are commonly raised on farms and grasslands.
  • They enjoy open areas where they can graze.
  • Sheep need clean water and safe shelter.
  • They usually stay together in a flock.
  • They can live in different climates when properly cared for.

🌿 What Does a Sheep Eat?

  • Grass 🌱
  • Hay
  • Leaves 🍃
  • Grains
  • Other green plants

🧠 Behaviour

  • Sheep are calm and social animals.
  • They usually stay close to their flock.
  • Sheep communicate using bleats and body movements.
  • They spend many hours grazing and eating.
  • Mother sheep carefully protect their lambs. 🥹

😂 Fun Facts

  • Baby sheep are called lambs.
  • Sheep wool is used to make many types of clothing.
  • Sheep can remember and recognize faces.
  • A group of sheep is called a flock.
  • Sheep have soft wool that keeps them warm.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–12 years
Weight: 45–160 kg
Height: 45–100 cm
Speed: Up to 20 km/h

🐰 Rabbit

🔬 Real Facts

  • Rabbits are small mammals with long ears and powerful back legs.
  • Baby rabbits are often called kits or kittens.
  • Rabbit teeth keep growing throughout their lives.
  • Rabbits have excellent hearing and a wide field of vision.
  • They can jump and run quickly when they feel threatened.

🏡 Habitat

  • Pet rabbits usually live in homes, hutches or safe outdoor areas.
  • Wild rabbits can live in grasslands, forests and open areas.
  • Rabbits need a clean, safe and comfortable place to rest.
  • They enjoy spaces where they can hop, explore and hide.
  • They need fresh water and plenty of food every day.

🥕 What Does a Rabbit Eat?

  • Hay 🌾
  • Grass 🌱
  • Leafy vegetables 🥬
  • Fresh vegetables 🥕
  • Small amounts of fruits 🍎

🧠 Behaviour

  • Rabbits are gentle and curious animals.
  • They enjoy hopping, exploring and playing.
  • Rabbits communicate using body movements and different sounds.
  • They groom themselves regularly to stay clean.
  • They can form strong bonds with people and other rabbits. 🥹

😂 Fun Facts

  • Rabbits can jump surprisingly high and far.
  • They can move their ears in different directions to detect sounds.
  • A rabbit's teeth never stop growing.
  • Baby rabbits are born with closed eyes and no fur.
  • Rabbits can do a happy jump called a "binky". 🥹🐰

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 8–12 years
Weight: 1–7 kg
Length: 20–50 cm
Speed: Up to 40 km/h

🐴 Horse

🔬 Real Facts

  • Horses are large mammals with strong legs and powerful bodies.
  • Horses have been domesticated by humans for thousands of years.
  • They have excellent hearing and a strong sense of smell.
  • Horses can sleep both standing up and lying down.
  • Baby horses are called foals. 🥹

🏡 Habitat

  • Domestic horses commonly live on farms, ranches and in stables.
  • They can live in grasslands, open fields and many other environments.
  • Horses need enough space to walk and exercise.
  • They need clean water, food and a safe shelter.
  • Horses enjoy spending time outdoors and grazing in open areas.

🌿 What Does a Horse Eat?

  • Grass 🌱
  • Hay 🌾
  • Grains
  • Carrots 🥕
  • Apples 🍎

🧠 Behaviour

  • Horses are social animals that enjoy living near other horses.
  • They communicate using sounds, facial expressions and body movements.
  • Horses can learn commands and recognize familiar people.
  • They can become nervous when they sense danger.
  • Horses often show affection through gentle contact and behaviour.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Horses can run shortly after being born.
  • A horse's eyes are among the largest of land mammals.
  • Horses can see in almost all directions around their body.
  • Horses can sleep while standing.
  • Baby horses are called foals and are usually very playful. 🥹🐴

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 25–30 years
Weight: 400–1,000 kg
Height: 1.4–1.8 m
Speed: Up to 70 km/h
