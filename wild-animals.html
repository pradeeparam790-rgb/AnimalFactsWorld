🐯 Tiger

🔬 Real Facts

  • Tigers are the largest members of the cat family.
  • Every tiger has a unique stripe pattern.
  • Tigers are excellent swimmers.
  • They are mostly active during early morning and evening.
  • Tigers usually live alone.

🌍 Habitat

  • Tigers live in forests, grasslands and mangrove areas.
  • They are found in parts of Asia.
  • They usually stay near water sources.
  • Dense vegetation helps them hide.
  • They need large areas to find food.

🍖 What Does a Tiger Eat?

  • Deer
  • Wild boar
  • Buffalo
  • Antelope
  • Other medium and large animals

🧠 Behaviour

  • Tigers usually live alone.
  • They protect their territory.
  • They use scent marks to communicate.
  • They can swim very well.
  • They are patient hunters.

😂 Fun Facts

  • No two tigers have exactly the same stripes.
  • Tigers enjoy swimming in water.
  • A tiger's stripes help it hide in vegetation.
  • Tigers can make different sounds to communicate.
  • They have powerful legs and sharp claws.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–15 years
Weight: 90–300 kg
Length: 2–3.3 m
Speed: Up to 65 km/h

🦁 Lion

🔬 Real Facts

  • Lions are large members of the cat family.
  • Male lions usually have a mane.
  • Lions are the only cats that commonly live in large social groups.
  • Lions are powerful predators.
  • A group of lions is called a pride.

🌍 Habitat

  • Lions mainly live in African grasslands and savannas.
  • They can also live in dry woodland areas.
  • They need water and prey nearby.
  • Open grasslands help them spot prey.
  • They often rest under shade during hot days.

🍖 What Does a Lion Eat?

  • Zebra
  • Wildebeest
  • Buffalo
  • Antelope
  • Wild boar

🧠 Behaviour

  • Lions usually live in groups called prides.
  • Females often hunt together.
  • Lions spend many hours resting.
  • They use roars to communicate.
  • They protect their territory from other lions.

😂 Fun Facts

  • A lion's roar can be heard from several kilometres away.
  • Male lions usually have larger manes than females.
  • Lions can rest for many hours each day.
  • Lions are famous for their powerful roar.
  • Lions are more commonly found in grasslands than jungles.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–15 years
Weight: 120–250 kg
Length: 1.7–2.5 m
Speed: Up to 80 km/h

🐘 Elephant

🔬 Real Facts

  • Elephants are the largest living land animals.
  • They have large ears and long trunks.
  • Their trunks are used for breathing, eating and drinking.
  • Elephants have strong social bonds.
  • They can communicate using sounds and vibrations.

🌍 Habitat

  • Elephants live in forests, grasslands and savannas.
  • African and Asian elephants live in different regions.
  • They need large areas to find food and water.
  • They can travel long distances in search of food.
  • They often stay near water sources.

🌿 What Does an Elephant Eat?

  • Grass
  • Leaves
  • Fruits
  • Tree bark
  • Roots and other plants

🧠 Behaviour

  • Elephants are highly social animals.
  • They usually live in family groups.
  • They care for young members of the group.
  • They use their trunks to touch and communicate.
  • They can form strong bonds with other elephants.

😂 Fun Facts

  • An elephant's trunk contains thousands of muscles.
  • Elephants can swim.
  • Their large ears help release body heat.
  • Elephants can use their trunks like a flexible hand.
  • They have very strong memories and social bonds.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 60–70 years
Weight: 2,700–6,000 kg
Height: 2.5–4 m
Speed: Up to 40 km/h

🐼 Giant Panda

🔬 Real Facts

  • Giant pandas are mammals from China.
  • They have distinctive black and white fur.
  • Pandas have strong jaws and teeth for chewing bamboo.
  • They have a special wrist bone that helps hold food.
  • Most of their day is spent eating and resting.

🌍 Habitat

  • Pandas live mainly in mountain forests of central China.
  • They prefer cool, damp forests.
  • Bamboo grows widely in their natural habitat.
  • They usually live at higher elevations.
  • They spend much of their time on the ground.

🎋 What Does a Panda Eat?

  • Bamboo leaves
  • Bamboo stems
  • Bamboo shoots
  • Small amounts of fruits
  • Occasionally small animals and eggs

🧠 Behaviour

  • Pandas usually live alone.
  • They spend many hours eating bamboo.
  • They can climb trees.
  • They are generally calm animals.
  • They communicate using sounds and scent marks.

😂 Fun Facts

  • A panda can spend around 10–16 hours a day eating.
  • Pandas have a thumb-like wrist bone.
  • Baby pandas are born very small compared with their mothers.
  • Pandas can climb trees to escape danger.
  • Despite their plant-based diet, pandas belong to the bear family.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 15–20 years
Weight: 70–120 kg
Length: 1.2–1.9 m
Food: Mostly bamboo

🐻 Bear

🔬 Real Facts

  • Bears are large mammals found in many parts of the world.
  • There are eight living species of bears.
  • Bears have strong bodies, powerful claws and excellent senses.
  • Most bears are good swimmers.
  • Bears can walk on the soles of their feet.

🌍 Habitat

  • Bears live in forests, mountains, grasslands and Arctic regions.
  • Different bear species live in different habitats.
  • They often choose areas with plenty of food.
  • Some bears build dens for resting or raising cubs.
  • Polar bears spend much of their lives around sea ice.

🍖 What Does a Bear Eat?

  • Fruits and berries
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Fish
  • Insects
  • Small and large animals

🧠 Behaviour

  • Most bears usually live alone.
  • They have an excellent sense of smell.
  • They can climb, swim and run depending on the species.
  • Some bears become less active during cold winter months.
  • Mother bears carefully protect their cubs.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Polar bears have black skin under their fur.
  • Some bears can run surprisingly fast.
  • Bears have very strong claws.
  • Some bear species are excellent tree climbers.
  • Pandas are also members of the bear family.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 20–30 years
Weight: 25–700 kg
Height: 0.6–2.8 m
Speed: Up to 56 km/h

🦓 Zebra

🔬 Real Facts

  • Zebras are hoofed mammals closely related to horses and donkeys.
  • Every zebra has a unique pattern of black and white stripes.
  • Zebras have strong legs that help them run quickly.
  • They usually live in groups called herds.
  • Zebras can communicate using sounds, body movements and facial expressions.

🌍 Habitat

  • Zebras mainly live in African grasslands and savannas.
  • Some species also live in mountainous or dry regions.
  • They prefer areas where grass and water are available.
  • They travel long distances to find food and water.
  • Open grasslands help zebras watch for predators.

🌿 What Does a Zebra Eat?

  • Grass
  • Leaves
  • Herbs
  • Shrubs
  • Other green plants

🧠 Behaviour

  • Zebras usually stay together in groups.
  • They use their ears and body movements to communicate.
  • They can run quickly when they sense danger.
  • Zebras may kick predators with their powerful back legs.
  • They spend a large part of the day feeding.

😂 Fun Facts

  • No two zebras have exactly the same stripe pattern.
  • A zebra's stripes may help confuse predators.
  • Zebras can sleep while standing for short periods.
  • Baby zebras can walk soon after they are born.
  • Zebras can run at high speeds when escaping danger.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 20–25 years
Weight: 175–450 kg
Height: 1.2–1.5 m
Speed: Up to 65 km/h

🦒 Giraffe

🔬 Real Facts

  • Giraffes are the tallest land animals in the world.
  • They have very long necks and legs.
  • Each giraffe has a unique pattern of spots.
  • Giraffes have long tongues that help them reach leaves.
  • They have small horn-like structures called ossicones on their heads.

🌍 Habitat

  • Giraffes mainly live in African savannas and grasslands.
  • They can also be found in open woodlands.
  • They prefer areas with plenty of trees and shrubs.
  • They can travel long distances to find food and water.
  • Their height helps them see across open areas.

🌿 What Does a Giraffe Eat?

  • Acacia leaves
  • Tree leaves
  • Shrubs
  • Flowers
  • Young shoots and other plants

🧠 Behaviour

  • Giraffes usually live in loose social groups.
  • They spend many hours of the day feeding.
  • They use their long tongues to pull leaves from trees.
  • They can run quickly when they sense danger.
  • Giraffes communicate using sounds and body movements.

😂 Fun Facts

  • A giraffe's neck can be over 2 metres long.
  • Giraffes have the same number of neck bones as humans — seven.
  • Their long tongues can be around 45 cm long.
  • Giraffes can sleep while standing for short periods.
  • Their unique spots help identify individual giraffes.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 20–25 years
Weight: 550–1,930 kg
Height: 4.3–5.7 m
Speed: Up to 60 km/h

🐒 Monkey

🔬 Real Facts

  • Monkeys are intelligent mammals belonging to the primate group.
  • There are many different species of monkeys around the world.
  • Many monkeys have strong hands and feet for climbing.
  • Some monkey species have long tails that help with balance.
  • Monkeys can learn, remember and solve simple problems.

🌍 Habitat

  • Monkeys live mainly in tropical and subtropical regions.
  • Many species live in forests and rainforests.
  • Some monkeys also live in grasslands, mountains and dry areas.
  • Tree branches provide food, shelter and protection for many species.
  • Different monkey species are found in Africa, Asia and the Americas.

🍌 What Does a Monkey Eat?

  • Fruits
  • Leaves
  • Seeds and nuts
  • Insects
  • Flowers and other plant parts

🧠 Behaviour

  • Many monkeys live in social groups.
  • They communicate using sounds, facial expressions and body movements.
  • They often groom one another to strengthen social bonds.
  • Many species are excellent climbers.
  • Mother monkeys carefully protect and care for their babies.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Some monkeys can use their tails to help them move through trees.
  • Monkeys have excellent vision.
  • Some monkeys use simple tools to get food.
  • They can make many different sounds to communicate.
  • Monkeys are closely related to apes and humans.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Lifespan: 10–30+ years
Weight: 0.1–50 kg
Height: Varies by species
Speed: Varies by species
