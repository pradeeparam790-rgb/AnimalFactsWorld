🌿 Meet Our Feathered Friends

Explore interesting facts, habitats, food habits, behaviour, fun facts, size and lifespan of some common and fascinating birds.

🦜 Parrot

🔬 Real Facts

  • Parrots are colourful birds known for their curved beaks.
  • Many parrot species can copy human speech and other sounds.
  • They have strong feet that help them hold food and climb.
  • Parrots are intelligent and can learn different sounds and tricks.
  • They are found in many warm regions around the world.

🌍 Habitat

  • Parrots commonly live in forests, woodlands and tropical areas.
  • Some species also live in grasslands and urban areas.
  • They usually stay in trees and use tree holes for nesting.

🍎 What Does It Eat?

  • Seeds
  • Fruits
  • Nuts
  • Berries
  • Flowers and plant parts

🧠 Behaviour

  • Parrots are social and often live in groups.
  • They communicate using calls, sounds and body movements.
  • Many parrots are playful and curious.
  • They use their strong beaks to open hard food.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Some parrots can learn and repeat many human words.
  • Parrots use their feet almost like hands while eating.
  • Their colourful feathers help make them easily recognisable.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Depends on the species
Lifespan: Around 10–60+ years depending on species

🦚 Peacock

🔬 Real Facts

  • The peacock is famous for its beautiful and colourful tail feathers.
  • The male peacock is called a peacock, while the female is called a peahen.
  • Peacocks spread their large tail feathers during courtship displays.
  • They are ground-dwelling birds but can fly short distances.
  • Peacocks are native to parts of Asia.

🌍 Habitat

  • Peacocks commonly live in forests and open woodland areas.
  • They can also live near farms, villages and gardens.
  • They usually rest and sleep in trees at night.

🌾 What Does It Eat?

  • Seeds
  • Fruits
  • Grains
  • Insects
  • Small plants and other small animals

🧠 Behaviour

  • Peacocks are active mainly during the day.
  • Male peacocks display their tail feathers to attract females.
  • They use loud calls to communicate with each other.
  • They often search for food by walking on the ground.

😂 Fun Facts

  • The colourful tail of a male peacock contains many eye-like patterns.
  • Peacocks can make surprisingly loud calls.
  • Despite their large tail, peacocks can fly and climb into trees.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Around 90–130 cm body length
Lifespan: Around 10–20 years

🦅 Eagle

🔬 Real Facts

  • Eagles are powerful birds of prey with strong, curved beaks.
  • They have excellent eyesight and can spot prey from far away.
  • Strong talons help them catch and carry their prey.
  • Eagles are found in many parts of the world.
  • Different eagle species vary greatly in size and appearance.

🌍 Habitat

  • Eagles can live near forests, mountains, rivers, lakes and coastal areas.
  • Many species build large nests high in trees or on cliffs.
  • They choose habitats where food is easily available.

🍖 What Does It Eat?

  • Fish
  • Small mammals
  • Birds
  • Reptiles
  • Other small animals

🧠 Behaviour

  • Eagles are skilled hunters.
  • They use their powerful wings to soar high in the sky.
  • They use their sharp talons to catch prey.
  • Some eagles spend a lot of time gliding and searching for food.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Eagles can see much farther than humans.
  • Some eagle species can soar for long periods without flapping their wings.
  • The bald eagle is the national bird of the United States.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Varies greatly by species
Lifespan: Around 20–30 years in many species

🦉 Owl

🔬 Real Facts

  • Owls are birds of prey known for their large eyes and silent flight.
  • They have excellent hearing and can locate prey using sound.
  • Most owl species are active during the night.
  • Owls have sharp talons that help them catch prey.
  • There are many different species of owls around the world.

🌍 Habitat

  • Owls can live in forests, grasslands, deserts and even cities.
  • They often rest in tree holes, caves or other sheltered places.
  • Different owl species are adapted to different environments.

🍖 What Does It Eat?

  • Mice and other small mammals
  • Small birds
  • Insects
  • Frogs
  • Small reptiles

🧠 Behaviour

  • Most owls are nocturnal, meaning they are active at night.
  • They can turn their heads a long way to look around.
  • Their special feathers help them fly quietly.
  • Owls often hunt by listening carefully for movement.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Owls cannot move their eyeballs like humans do.
  • Instead, they move their heads to look in different directions.
  • Some owl species can hunt almost silently because of their special wing feathers.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Varies greatly by species
Lifespan: Around 5–20+ years depending on species

🦢 Swan

🔬 Real Facts

  • Swans are large water birds with long necks and graceful bodies.
  • They are known for their elegant swimming and beautiful appearance.
  • Swans are found in different parts of the world.
  • They have strong webbed feet that help them swim.
  • There are several species of swans, and their size and colour can vary.

🌍 Habitat

  • Swans commonly live in lakes, ponds, rivers and wetlands.
  • They spend much of their time in or near water.
  • Some swans migrate between breeding and wintering areas.

🌿 What Does It Eat?

  • Aquatic plants
  • Grass and other vegetation
  • Seeds
  • Small aquatic animals
  • Small insects and other tiny organisms

🧠 Behaviour

  • Swans are social birds and can often be seen swimming in pairs or groups.
  • Many swan species form long-term pair bonds with their partners.
  • Both parents can take part in caring for their young.
  • Swans communicate using calls, body movements and displays.

❤️ Love & Bonding

  • Many swans form strong, long-term bonds with a partner.
  • Pairs often stay together through breeding seasons and may remain together for many years.
  • They can be seen swimming and moving together as a pair.
  • Both parents may help protect and care for their cygnets.
  • This strong bonding is one reason swans are often used as a symbol of love and loyalty.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Baby swans are called cygnets.
  • Swans can use their long necks to reach underwater plants.
  • Despite their graceful appearance, swans can be very protective of their nests.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Around 1–1.5 metres in body length
Lifespan: Often around 10–20+ years

🐦 Pigeon

🔬 Real Facts

  • Pigeons are common birds found in cities, villages and natural areas.
  • They have excellent navigation abilities and can find their way home.
  • Pigeons can fly quickly and manoeuvre through crowded places.
  • They are intelligent birds that can learn and remember patterns.
  • Rock pigeons are the ancestors of many domestic pigeon varieties.

🌍 Habitat

  • Pigeons commonly live in cities, towns, farms and rocky areas.
  • They often build nests on buildings, bridges and ledges.
  • They are highly adaptable to human environments.

🌾 What Does It Eat?

  • Seeds
  • Grains
  • Fruits
  • Small plant materials
  • Other available food in urban areas

🧠 Behaviour

  • Pigeons are social birds and often live in groups.
  • They communicate using calls and body movements.
  • They can remember familiar places and routes.
  • Both parents can help care for their chicks.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Pigeons have been used to carry messages over long distances.
  • They have a strong ability to recognise familiar locations.
  • Pigeons can find their way home from unfamiliar places.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Around 30–35 cm in length
Lifespan: Around 3–6 years in the wild

🐦 Sparrow

🔬 Real Facts

  • Sparrows are small, active birds commonly seen near people.
  • They have short, strong beaks that are useful for eating seeds.
  • House sparrows are one of the most familiar sparrow species.
  • They can live successfully in both rural and urban areas.
  • Sparrows are usually quick and energetic birds.

🌍 Habitat

  • Sparrows commonly live around houses, gardens, farms and cities.
  • They often build nests in small spaces in buildings and other structures.
  • They can also be found around trees, bushes and open areas.

🌾 What Does It Eat?

  • Seeds
  • Grains
  • Small fruits
  • Insects
  • Small plant materials

🧠 Behaviour

  • Sparrows are active and social birds.
  • They often search for food together in small groups.
  • They communicate with short calls and chirping sounds.
  • During breeding season, both parents may help care for their chicks.

😂 Fun Facts

  • Sparrows are excellent at adapting to human environments.
  • Their small size helps them move quickly through bushes and buildings.
  • Sparrows can take dust baths to help keep their feathers clean.

📏 Size & Lifespan

Size: Around 14–18 cm in length
Lifespan: Often around 3–5 years in the wild
